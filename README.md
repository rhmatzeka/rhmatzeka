### Open source

When a tool I use has a bug, I try to fix it upstream instead of working around it. That got my first patches into [Hyperledger Fabric](https://github.com/hyperledger/fabric/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged), where I fixed a peer crash in gossip discovery, and into [Fabric-X](https://github.com/hyperledger/fabric-x-orderer/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged). I've sent 9 pull requests to AMD's [GAIA](https://github.com/amd/gaia/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged) and 11 to the XSS scanner [Dalfox](https://github.com/hahwul/dalfox/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged), and one change to [gortex](https://github.com/zzet/gortex/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged) brought its indexer's peak memory down from 968 MB to 503 MB. More recently I fixed swapped `calloc` arguments in Khronos' [OpenCL ICD Loader](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/OpenCL-ICD-Loader/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged), corrected a page on [ethereum.org](https://github.com/ethereum/ethereum-org-website/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged), packaged 9 Linux apps for [deb-get](https://github.com/wimpysworld/deb-get/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged) and two more for [Scoop](https://github.com/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+org%3AScoopInstaller+is%3Amerged) on Windows. Smaller fixes went into [viem](https://github.com/wevm/viem/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged), [repowise](https://github.com/repowise-dev/repowise/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged), [CrossMacro](https://github.com/alper-han/CrossMacro/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged) and [ECC](https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC/pulls?q=is%3Apr+author%3Arhmatzeka+is%3Amerged).
